# Briefs

## Reading a brief

Extract, in this order, and write each one down even when the brief is silent:

1. **Objective** — what changes if the campaign works (sign-ups, sales, awareness).
   A brief without a measurable objective is incomplete; say so and propose one.
2. **Audience** — who, described by situation and motivation, not by demographics alone.
3. **Single message** — the one sentence the audience should repeat afterwards.
4. **Proof** — why they should believe it (product facts, evidence, social proof).
5. **Constraints** — budget, dates, channels already decided, legal or brand limits.
6. **Brand voice** — tone words and forbidden words from the brand guide, if any
   was provided under `data/`.

## The strategy document

`artifacts/strategy.md` has these headings, in this order:

- `# Estrategia de campaña` and the objective as the first line.
- `## Audiencia` — one primary audience, optionally one secondary.
- `## Mensaje` — the single message and two supporting points.
- `## Canales` — in priority order, each with one sentence of rationale.
- `## Lo que no haremos` — explicit exclusions, so scope stays honest.
- `## Riesgos` — claims that need proof, tone risks, timing risks.

Keep it under two pages. A strategy a copywriter cannot act on without questions
is not finished.
