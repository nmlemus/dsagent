# Copy

## Per channel

| Channel | Shape | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Email | subject + preheader + body + one CTA | subject ≤ 50 chars, body ≤ 150 words |
| Social post | hook line + body + CTA, optional hashtags | ≤ 280 chars for short networks |
| Landing hero | headline + subheadline + CTA | headline ≤ 8 words |
| Paid ad | 3 headlines + 2 descriptions | headline ≤ 30 chars, description ≤ 90 chars |

## Structure of `deliverables/campaign-copy.md`

- `# Copy de campaña` with the objective and the audience on the first two lines.
- One `## <Canal>` section per channel in the strategy's priority order.
- Inside each: the recommended piece first, then at most two alternatives that
  differ in *idea*, each with a one-line rationale.
- `## Notas para revisión` — any claim that needs proof, any place where the
  strategy and the brand guide conflict.

## Checklist before delivering

- The single message from the strategy appears, recognisably, in every channel.
- No forbidden word or claim from the brand guide.
- Every piece has exactly one call to action.
- Lengths respect the table above; count, do not estimate.
